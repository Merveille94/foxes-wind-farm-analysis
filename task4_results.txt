Excerpt from the slurm outputfiles from HPC computations:

considering all 5143 turbines:

AOI mean power (with wakes) : 1196.6 MW
AOI mean power (ambient/no wake): 1514.9 MW
AOI efficiency   : 78.99 %
AOI annual yield (with wakes)   : 10482.11 GWh

considering 2014 turbines within 50km of AOI:

AOI mean power (with wakes) : 1208.1 MW
AOI mean power (ambient/no wake): 1514.9 MW
AOI efficiency   : 79.75 %
AOI annual yield (with wakes)   : 10582.91 GWh



Wake losses are 21.01 % considering all turbines vs. 20.25 % considering turbines within 50km.


Simulating only the AOI gives the result for only the internal wake effects:

Farm power        : 1300.7 MW
Farm ambient power: 1514.9 MW
Farm efficiency   : 85.86 %
Annual farm yield : 11394.26 GWh

This lets us conlcude that most wake losses actually stem from internal wake effects (14.14 %).
External wake effects only contribute 21.01 % - 14.14 % = 6.87 % of the total wake losses.

Here, considering turbines within 50km of teh AOI is sufficient since turbines further away 
contributed less than 1 % of the wake losses while significantly increasing computational cost.
